🔒 Our Commitment

BABASITARAM PRO does not operate any backend server, and the developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any of your data.

Settings, preferences, and locally generated statistics are stored on your device. Browser extensions use built-in storage APIs such as chrome.storage.local. Passwords and vault data in the Android app are encrypted locally using AES-256. Optional browser sync and the third-party requests described below are exceptions to purely local processing; none send data to a developer-operated server.

We do not collect:

  • ❌ Personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • ❌ Browsing history
  • ❌ Usage statistics
  • ❌ Analytics or telemetry
  • ❌ Cookies or credentials
  • ❌ Health, financial, or authentication data
  • ❌ Your real location or GPS data (Location Guard BSRPRO never reads or stores it)

No account is required. No tracking. No ads. 100% private.

📍 About Location Guard BSRPRO

Location Guard BSRPRO lets you choose a location and reports that location to websites instead of your device's real location.

  • The extension never reads, stores, or sends your real location.
  • Only the location you choose yourself is saved, locally on your device.
  • It changes only the browser's Geolocation API. It does not hide your IP address; websites can still estimate your general area from your IP.
  • It does not run on browser-internal pages.

☁️ Browser Sync (Optional)

If you enable Cloud Sync in a supporting extension:

  • Sync is provided by your browser vendor (such as Google, Microsoft, or Mozilla), not the developer.
  • It operates only when enabled and syncs through your signed-in browser environment.

Location Guard BSRPRO does not use browser sync. Its saved settings remain in local storage.

🌐 Network Requests These Extensions Make

1. Filter List Updates (Automatic)

Ultimate Privacy Shield periodically downloads published ad/tracker block-lists:

  • EasyList (easylist.to)
  • EasyPrivacy (easylist.to)
  • uBlock Origin Annoyances (ublockorigin.com)

These requests download public lists and do not intentionally transmit your browsing history. The hosting services may receive ordinary request metadata such as your IP address.

2. Breach Alert (Optional, User-Initiated)

If you press Check in Breach Alert, the email address you enter is sent directly to the Have I Been Pwned API, not to the developer. You must supply your own HIBP API key; see HIBP API key information. The API provider may also receive normal network metadata and authentication headers.

3. DNS-over-HTTPS (Optional)

If enabled, your browser uses its native DNS-over-HTTPS functionality with the provider you select (for example, Cloudflare, Google, or Quad9). DNS queries are processed by that provider, not by the developer.

4. Map and Place Search (Location Guard BSRPRO Only)

When you open the popup and use the map or place search:

  • Map tiles load from tile.openstreetmap.org, with fallback to basemaps.cartocdn.com (CARTO) or server.arcgisonline.com (Esri). Requests reflect the displayed map area.
  • Place search and name lookup use nominatim.openstreetmap.org and send your entered search text or selected coordinates.
  • These providers receive standard technical request data, including your IP address and browser information, subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer, and your device's real GPS location is not included in these requests.
